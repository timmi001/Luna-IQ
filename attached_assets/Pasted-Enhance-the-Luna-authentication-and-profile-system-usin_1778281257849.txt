Enhance the Luna authentication and profile system using Supabase.

The app should collect:
- full name
- date of birth (DOB)

during onboarding/signup.

Requirements:

---

## PROFILE SETUP

Update the profiles table to include:

- full_name
- first_name
- date_of_birth
- birthday_last_shown_at
- created_at

Automatically extract the user’s first name from their full name.

Example:
- “Sarah Johnson” → first_name = “Sarah”

Store both values in Supabase.

---

## DYNAMIC PERSONALIZED GREETINGS

Use the user’s first name dynamically throughout the app.

Examples:
- “Good evening, Sarah 💜”
- “How are you feeling today, Sarah?”
- “Welcome back, Sarah 🌸”

The greeting system should:
- change based on time of day
- feel soft, calming, and emotionally warm
- update dynamically after login

Time examples:
- Morning
- Afternoon
- Evening

---

## BIRTHDAY EXPERIENCE

Use the user’s DOB to create a birthday experience.

On the user’s birthday:
- show a special birthday greeting
- display a soft celebratory UI moment
- optionally reward bonus Luna Points

Examples:
- “Happy Birthday, Sarah 💜”
- “Wishing you a gentle and beautiful year ahead 🌸”

Requirements:
- birthday message should appear only once per birthday/day
- avoid showing repeatedly on every refresh
- store last shown date in Supabase

---

## OPTIONAL BIRTHDAY REWARD

Add optional bonus:
- +50 Luna Points on birthday

Display:
“Birthday reward unlocked 💜”

Ensure:
- reward can only happen once per year
